## Preparation

Cut foam sheets to 5 × 7 inches. Set up one inking station for every six to eight students with a slab, a brayer, and a small amount of ink.

## Steps

1. Students draw lightly on scrap paper first, then redraw the design on the foam, pressing firmly enough to make a groove without tearing through.
2. Remind students that pressed lines print white and that any lettering will come out backwards.
3. At the inking station, roll ink thin and even, then roll it across the foam.
4. Lay paper on the foam and rub with a flat hand or the back of a spoon.
5. Peel slowly and set the print on the drying rack or a clear counter.
6. Re-ink and print two or three more times, trying different paper colors.

## Cleanup

Wipe foam plates with a damp paper towel if students will reuse them. Wash brayers and slabs with warm water.

## Assessment and reflection

Ask students why some lines came out crisper than others. Look for an understanding of the raised surface printing.

## Extensions

Cut the foam into two pieces and ink them in different colors before reassembling them for printing.
