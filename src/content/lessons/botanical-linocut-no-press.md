## What changes from the original

Sessions 1 and 2 are the same as the original lesson. In session 3, students print by hand.

## Hand printing

Thinner paper works better without a press, so copy paper or a light printmaking paper gives fuller prints than heavy stock. Lay the paper on the inked block, hold it still with one hand, and rub in small overlapping circles with the barren or the back of a wooden spoon. Lift one corner to check coverage before peeling the whole sheet.

## Classroom note

Burnishing is tiring, so pair students and let them take turns holding the paper steady.
