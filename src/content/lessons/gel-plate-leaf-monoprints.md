## Preparation

Collect flat leaves the day before and press them under a heavy book. Cover tables with newsprint.

## Steps

1. Roll a thin layer of acrylic paint across the gel plate.
2. Lay leaves on the paint, vein side down.
3. Press paper on top, rub with a flat hand, and pull. This print shows the background with leaf shapes left blank.
4. Lift the leaves off the plate. The paint left behind holds the leaf texture. Lay a fresh sheet down to print that ghost image.
5. Try a second color on top of a dry first print.

## Cleanup

Wipe the plate with a baby wipe or damp towel while the paint is still wet.

## Assessment and reflection

Ask students to compare the first and second pull and describe the difference between the two.
