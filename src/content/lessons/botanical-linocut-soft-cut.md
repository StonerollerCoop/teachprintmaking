## What changes from the original

Students use 3 × 4 inch soft-cut blocks and carve with a single U-gouge. Drawing and carving happen in the first session, and printing happens in the second.

## Session 1

Draw one leaf or flower in thick outline. Transfer it to the block and carve away the background.

## Session 2

Ink with a brayer and print with a barren or spoon. Each student pulls at least two prints and chooses the stronger one to display.
