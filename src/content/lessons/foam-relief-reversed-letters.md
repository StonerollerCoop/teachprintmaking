## What changes from the original

Students print their name or initials. Let them draw it forwards on the foam first and print it, so the backwards result makes the point for them.

## Steps

1. Students write their name forwards on the foam and make one print.
2. Talk about what happened. Then show how to reverse the drawing by writing on thin paper, flipping it over, and tracing through.
3. Students draw a baseline on the second plate before reversing their letters onto it.
4. Print the reversed version and compare the two.

## Reflection

Ask what printers had to know when every piece of type was made by hand.
