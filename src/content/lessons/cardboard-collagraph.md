## Preparation

Cut cardboard bases to 6 × 8 inches. Gather texture scraps such as lace, mesh bags, corrugated cardboard, leaves, and string.

## Session 1: Building the plate

Students glue cardboard shapes and textures to the base, keeping everything fairly flat. Brush a thin coat of diluted glue or acrylic medium over the whole plate to seal it. Plates dry overnight.

## Session 2: Printing

Ink with a brayer, lay paper over the plate, and burnish firmly, pressing paper into the textures with fingertips as well. Pull two or three prints.

## Cleanup

Plates can be kept and re-inked. Wash brayers and slabs with water.

## Assessment and reflection

Ask which textures printed best and why some barely printed at all.
