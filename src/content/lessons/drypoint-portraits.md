## Why there is no no-press adaptation

Drypoint needs heavy, even pressure to push dampened paper into fine scratched lines. Hand burnishing does not reliably do this, so this lesson is not offered as an adaptation when no press is available. A trace monotype is a better choice for a no-press classroom.

## Preparation

Cut plates to 5 × 7 inches and file the edges. Tear paper to size and soak it for at least 20 minutes before printing, then blot it between towels.

## Session 1: Drawing the plate

Students place a reference photo or drawing under the clear plate and scratch lines with the needle. Show how cross-hatching and dense lines build darker areas.

## Session 2: Inking, wiping, and printing

Spread ink over the plate with a card, push it into the lines, then wipe the surface with tarlatan or newsprint until the lines stand out. Print on the press with dampened paper and blankets.

## Cleanup

Water-washable intaglio ink cleans up with soap and water. Wash the soaking tray and hang the blankets to dry.

## Assessment and reflection

Ask students to describe how they made the darkest areas and what wiping did to the tone.
