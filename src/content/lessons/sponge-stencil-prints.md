## Preparation

Cut simple shapes such as circles, stars, and leaves out of the middle of cardstock squares. Put small amounts of paint on paper plates.

## Steps

1. Show how to hold the stencil flat with one hand.
2. Dab the sponge in paint, then dab it up and down through the opening. Avoid wiping, which pushes paint under the edge.
3. Lift the stencil straight up.
4. Move the stencil and repeat to make a pattern.

## Cleanup

Collect sponges in a bucket of water. Stencils can dry and be used again.

## Reflection

Ask children what happened to the paint that landed on the stencil instead of the paper.
