## Preparation

Slip a piece of cardboard inside each tote so ink does not soak through to the back.

## Session 1: Stencils

Students design a bold image with no tiny islands, then cut it out of copy paper. Practice printing on paper first.

## Session 2: Printing

Tape the stencil under the screen. Lay the screen on the tote, spoon a line of ink above the image, and pull the squeegee toward you at a steady angle. Lift the screen straight up.

## Cleanup

Wash screens right away with water and a soft brush. Ink that dries in the mesh can ruin a screen.

## Reflection

Ask students how the pressure and angle of the squeegee changed the print.
