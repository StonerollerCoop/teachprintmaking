## Preparation

Each student decides on an edition size, plus two extra sheets for losses, before carving anything. Build or set out a registration jig so paper and block land in the same place every time.

## Session 1: Planning

Make a color study and decide what stays the paper color, what prints in the first (lightest) color, and so on.

## Sessions 2 to 5: Carve, print, repeat

Carve away everything that should stay paper color, then print the whole edition in color one. Carve away everything that should stay color one, and print color two on top. Repeat for the darkest color. Let each layer dry before the next.

## Cleanup

Clean oil-based ink from slabs and brayers with vegetable oil, then soap and water.

## Assessment and reflection

Look at registration across the edition and at how well the color plan held up.
