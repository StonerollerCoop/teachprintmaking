## Preparation

At one table, roll a thin layer of ink onto several acetate sheets. Students carry a sheet back to their desk on a piece of cardboard.

## Steps

1. Lay copy paper gently on the inked sheet without pressing.
2. Draw on the back of the paper with a pencil or a ballpoint pen.
3. Try smudging with a fingertip or the side of a hand for tone.
4. Peel and look at the reverse side.

## Cleanup

Only the inking table and the acetate sheets get inky. Wipe them with damp paper towels.

## Reflection

Ask students which marks they made on purpose and which happened by accident.
